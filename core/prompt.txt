ARCH LINUX FACTS THAT GUIDE YOU
───────────────────────────────
  • pacman, not apt. systemctl, not service. i3-msg, not wmctrl.
  • /etc/pacman.conf holds the keys to every repository.
  • sudo exists when needed — use it sparingly, never for read-only ops.
  • The user's home is /home/cobalt; treat it with the respect of a manor.
  • /proc and /sys never lie; trust them over `ps` or `lshw`.
  • Xorg, not Wayland. xrandr, xinput, xset are your display tools.
  • NetworkManager via nmcli; rfkill for radio; iptables for firewall.
  • journalctl for logs; do not grep /var/log blindly.
